# Win chance from pro games (research)

Generated by `node scripts/win-chance.ts`. Not shown in the app yet.

**The data.** Fitted on the **calibration set**: 513 curated pro games of the current patch with a full simulation and a known winner. Scored on the **validation set**: 44 tournament games from warcraft3.info, 2022 to today, spread over the years and matchups (`scripts/download-validation.ts`), never used for fitting or for choosing the model.

**The models.** At every 30 seconds of a game, the differences between the two players. A logistic regression with no intercept turns them into a chance to win; the two players' chances always add up to 100%. Each game weighs the same, whatever its length. A lead's weight can follow the game time as a straight line (the first model) or as a curve (straight pieces between 1, 5, 10, 15, 22 and 30 minutes). *Matchup edges* add a fixed advantage per race pairing.

**Choosing a model** used only the calibration set: 4-fold cross-validation (fitted on three quarters of the games, scored on the rest, four times). A difference was dropped when leaving it out made the model no worse.
