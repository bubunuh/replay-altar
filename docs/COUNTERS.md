# Counters tested on pro games

Generated by `node scripts/test-counters.ts` from 472 pro games (warcraft3.info tournament players, current patch at the time they were collected) and their 2395 fights between the players (creep fights and fights with under 200 resources lost in all left out).

**How to read it.** Each claim comes from a guide. *Trade share* is what the other side lost in a fight divided by what both lost: 50% is an even trade. *With* / *without*: the side's average trade share in fights where the opponent had the countered unit, with and without the counter in its own army (units ordered in the 6 minutes before the fight). *Difference*: with − without, in points, with a 90% range from resampling whole games. *Specific*: the same difference minus the one measured in fights where the opponent did **not** have the countered unit: a counter that is really about that unit helps more when that unit is there. Better players may also pick the counter more often, which these numbers can't separate.

## The game's damage table

For every fight, each army's average damage multiplier against the other's armor (the game's attack-type × armor-type table, weighted by unit cost; 1.0 = full damage; fighting units only, without spellcasters), and the difference between the two sides. If attack and armor types matter in practice, the side with the better multiplier should trade better.
